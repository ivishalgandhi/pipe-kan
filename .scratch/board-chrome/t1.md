## What to build

Cards show the assignee (avatar with the person's name) and label capsules whenever the list payload has them. Today the Card already paints those fields when they are present; live Boards often have neither because mapping only accepts `assignee.displayName` and can miss other jira-cli shapes. Restore the existing Card contract. Do not invent empty avatars or empty label slots.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An Issue whose assignee is `{ displayName }` still becomes a Card with that assignee.
- [ ] An Issue whose assignee has `name` but no `displayName` becomes a Card with that name.
- [ ] An Issue whose assignee is a non-empty string becomes a Card with that string.
- [ ] An Issue with no assignee, or an empty assignee, has no assignee on the Card (no empty avatar).
- [ ] Labels on the Card still come from `labels` plus `components`, de-duplicated, omitted when both are empty.
- [ ] The list invocation does not grow extra flags or columns.
- [ ] Tests follow the existing Board mapping tests: the three assignee shapes above, plus labels/components present and absent.
